built-in ImageGen tool. The website pairs it with warm paper, charcoal and

Transparent background. No text, no letters, no numbers, no stars, no hammer and
sickle, no flags, no watermark. Single emblem, not a sheet of variants. Suitable
as the hero brand asset on an ivory and black minimalist technical website.
